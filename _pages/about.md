**Xilong Cheng** received his Master’s degree from the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Communication University of China, in 2026, under the supervision of **[Yunxiao Qin](https://qyxqyx.github.io/homepage/)**. He currently works on multimodal large language models at [Mininglamp Technology](https://www.mininglamp.com/). He is also a remote visiting scholar with the **[X-NLP Lab](https://xnlp-lab.insait.ai/)** at INSAIT, collaborating closely with **[Yuxia Wang](https://yuxiaw.github.io/)**. He is dedicated to making more intelligent, safer personalized AI accessible to more people.

His research interests include AI personalization, long-term memory, and learning from the real world through egocentric vision.
